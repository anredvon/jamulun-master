# Friendly Money Check Copy Design

## Intent

Make the Home entry and quiz selection screen clearly feel like a friendly set of short money habit quizzes, not a formal financial assessment or old-fashioned fortune check.

## Approved direction

- Use conversational Korean and a consistent `… 알아보기` pattern for quiz titles.
- Name the Home entry `나의 돈 습관 알아보기` and route it to the three-choice check hub.
- Use `나를 알아보는 머니 테스트` as the hub label and a question-shaped headline.
- Name the three quizzes `나의 돈 관리 습관 알아보기`, `나의 소비 스타일 알아보기`, and `오늘의 금전운 알아보기`.
- Keep the existing four-tab navigation unchanged until its revised structure is agreed.

## Approaches

- Keep `재물 흐름 체크`: rejected because it sounds dated and does not clearly describe a quiz.
- Use `나의 돈 습관 알아보기`: chosen because it is approachable and covers the hub's different money-related topics.
- Use English `Check`: rejected because it keeps the assessment-like tone and mixes languages.

## Constraints

- Preserve the three existing quiz destinations and their questionnaire behavior.
- Do not change bottom navigation in this pass.
- Keep the light entertainment and self-reflection framing; do not imply financial advice or guaranteed outcomes.

## Acceptance

- The Home tile opens the three-choice check hub.
- Home, hub heading, and quiz titles use the approved copy and friendly tone.
- Each of the three existing quiz links remains intact.
